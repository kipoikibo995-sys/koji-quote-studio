Free bonus: our playbook

5 tips for quote Shorts people actually finish

Use these with Quote Studio, or with any tool.

1

Hook in the first line

Put the most striking phrase on page one. Viewers decide in a second or two whether to keep watching.

2

One idea per page

Split long passages at natural pauses. Shorter pages read faster, feel more cinematic, and keep people watching.

3

Match the pace to the emotion

Use 4 seconds for punchy lines and 8 seconds for lines that need to land. If a page feels rushed, it is.

4

Always credit the source

End with the book and author. It's respectful, it builds trust, and it starts conversations in the comments.

5

Post in series, not one-offs

A recognisable format, with the same background and the same rhythm, trains your audience to look for your next video.

Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need video editing experience?

No. If you can type a quote and click a button, you can make a video. Every animation and transition is preset and tuned for readability.

Is this an AI tool?

No. Quote Studio doesn't call any AI or external service. You write the words, and your browser animates and encodes the video locally.

Where is my video rendered? Is my text uploaded?

Everything happens on your device. Nothing is uploaded, so your drafts and unpublished quotes stay private.

Which browser should I use?

Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop gives the best results and real MP4 output. If MP4 isn't supported, the studio automatically exports WebM instead.

Can I use quotes from published books?

You're responsible for the text you use. Short quotes with clear attribution are common on quote channels, but check the rules for your platform and region. For your own writing, you're free to go.

Can I use it on my phone?

The studio opens on mobile, but rendering is heavy work. For the most reliable results, use Chrome or Edge on a laptop or desktop.

Will there be a watermark on my video?

No. The videos you render are yours to post.

Can I add music or voice-over?

Not inside the studio yet. Most creators add a trending sound directly in the TikTok, YouTube Shorts or Reels app when uploading, which also helps reach.

Are my projects saved?

The current version doesn't have accounts or cloud storage. Keep your quotes in a notes app and paste them in when you're ready to render.

How long does rendering take?

It's roughly real-time. A 6-page video at 6 seconds per page takes a little over half a minute on a typical laptop. Longer videos take longer.
